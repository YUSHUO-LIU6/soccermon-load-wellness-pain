# Wellness polarity evidence note

The independent polarity audit is distributed as `WELLNESS_POLARITY_INDEPENDENT_AUDIT.csv`. The audit records the external source, the source wording or coding anchor, the transformation used in the analysis, the consistency judgment, confidence, and whether outcome data were used. All seven audited codings were classified as CONSISTENT with HIGH confidence, and the audit did not use outcome data.

The original freeze also retained local cached copies of third-party source documents and a screenshot used during provenance checking. Those cache files are not redistributed in this submission archive. The source identifiers and audit conclusions required to verify the coding are retained in the CSV, and the manuscript cites the underlying public sources directly.
